About STEM Simulators

STEMSimulators.org provides free, interactive, browser-based science visualizations and self-checking practice modules designed specifically for students, educators, and lifelong learners.

Our mission is to make abstract scientific concepts—such as chemical reaction mechanisms and atomic electron configurations—intuitive through real-time visual experimentation, interactive drills, and instant feedback without requiring downloads or complex software installations.

Whether used in a classroom laboratory or for independent review at home, these tools bridge the gap between static textbook problem sets and active digital exploration.
