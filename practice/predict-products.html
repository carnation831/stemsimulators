Predicting Products

Reactants in. You predict the products and their states.

Chemistry reference: products and states

How to type an answer

Type the products only, with a state on each one. Separate products with +. Order does not matter. Example: Zn(s) + CuSO₄(aq) → you type ZnSO4(aq) + Cu(s).

Plain numbers and subscripts both work (H2O or H₂O). Capitals are corrected when we check. A coefficient in front, such as the 2 in 2H2O(l), is ignored. Use parentheses when a polyatomic ion needs a subscript: Fe(NO3)2, Al2(SO4)3.

States of matter

  • (s) solid metals (except mercury), ionic salts with no water, and precipitates.
  • (l) liquid water in this course, and bromine, Br₂.
  • (g) H₂, N₂, O₂, F₂, Cl₂, CO₂, NH₃, H₂S.
  • (aq) dissolved in water. Mercury metal is Hg(l). Iodine is I₂(s).

Water on this page

  • Neutralization, peroxide, and acid reactions in water: H₂O(l) only.
  • Complete combustion, and H₂ + O₂: prefer H₂O(l). H₂O(g) is also accepted.
  • A heated hydrate: prefer H₂O(g). H₂O(l) is also accepted.
  • Bromine or iodine made in water: prefer Br₂(l) and I₂(s). (aq) is also accepted.

Five patterns

  • Synthesis: one product. Metal + nonmetal, or an oxide + water.
  • Decomposition: carbonates (not Group 1) → oxide + CO₂. Chlorates → chloride + O₂. Hydrates lose water. Some binaries split into elements.
  • Single replacement: an element replaces an ion below it on the activity series.
  • Double replacement: partners swap. A reaction needs a solid, a gas, or water.
  • Combustion: complete burning makes CO₂(g) and water.

Activity series

Most reactive to least: Li K Ba Ca Na Mg Al Zn Fe Ni Sn Pb (H) Cu Ag Au.

A metal replaces ions below it. It replaces hydrogen in an acid only if it is above H. Halogens: F₂ > Cl₂ > Br₂ > I₂.

Iron metal with an acid, Cu²⁺, or Ag⁺ makes Fe²⁺. Iron with Cl₂ makes FeCl₃. Tin with HCl makes SnCl₂.

Solubility, using cold-water rules

  • Soluble (aq): Li⁺, Na⁺, K⁺, NH₄⁺, and NO₃⁻ salts. Most Cl⁻, Br⁻, and I⁻ salts, except Ag⁺ and Pb²⁺. Most SO₄²⁻ salts, except Ba²⁺, Pb²⁺, Ca²⁺, and Sr²⁺.
  • Insoluble (s): most CO₃²⁻, PO₄³⁻, OH⁻, and S²⁻ compounds, except with Li⁺, Na⁺, K⁺, or NH₄⁺. Group 1 hydroxides are (aq).
  • Gases: acid + carbonate → salt + H₂O(l) + CO₂(g). Acid + sulfide → salt + H₂S(g). Ammonium salt + hydroxide → salt + NH₃(g) + H₂O(l). NaCl(aq) + NH₄OH(aq) is also accepted for that last case.
No reaction is only in Mixed Practice Level 2. Type no reaction or NR when a metal or halogen is too unreactive, or when a double replacement would make two soluble salts and no gas or water.
