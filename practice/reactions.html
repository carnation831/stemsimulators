⚡ Chemistry Reference Guide: Bonding, Reaction Patterns & States of Matter

🔵 Ionic Compounds (Metal/Ammonium + Nonmetal)

Starts with a Metal (or NH₄⁺) paired with nonmetals or polyatomic ions.

Common Metals: Na, K, Ca, Mg, Ba, Ag, Cu, Zn, Pb, Fe, Al

🟣 Covalent Molecules (Nonmetal + Nonmetal)

Made ONLY of Nonmetals (upper right + Hydrogen).

Common Nonmetals: H, C, N, O, F, Cl, Br, I, S, P

🧪 Level 2: Predicting Product States of Matter — (s), (l), (g), (aq)

  • (s) Solids: Pure metals (Fe, Cu, Ag, Mg, Al), ionic oxides/salts formed without water (Fe₂O₃, Na₂CO₃), and insoluble precipitates (BaSO₄, CaSO₄, AgCl, Al(OH)₃, CaCO₃).
  • (l) Liquids: Pure water (H₂O) and elemental bromine (Br₂) at room temperature.
  • (g) Gases: Diatomic gases (H₂, O₂, N₂, F₂, Cl₂), carbon dioxide (CO₂), and ammonia (NH₃).
  • (aq) Aqueous: Soluble ionic compounds in water—all Group 1 (Na⁺, K⁺), Ammonium (NH₄⁺), and Nitrate (NO₃⁻) salts plus soluble acids (H₃PO₄) are (aq)!
⚠️ Lone Metals: A single unbonded metal element (Na, Zn, Cu, Ag, Fe, Mg, Al) is a pure metallic element—neither ionic nor covalent!
