Ionic vs Covalent

Look at one compound. Label each part as a metal or a nonmetal, then decide if the compound is ionic or covalent. Level 1 is binary compounds. Level 2 adds polyatomic ions, including ammonium.

Types of Reactions

Look at a whole reaction. Count the reactants and the products, then name the type: synthesis, decomposition, single replacement, double replacement, or combustion. Level 3 also asks for the state of each product.
