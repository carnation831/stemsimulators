In a controlled experiment, the Independent Variable (IV) is what the scientist intentionally manipulates, while the Dependent Variable (DV) is what is measured in response.

Assign your variables to the horizontal (X-axis) and vertical (Y-axis) axes. Always include measurement units in parentheses and write a descriptive scientific title.

Choose a clean, uniform scale for each axis so your data covers more than 50% of the grid without awkward decimals (use friendly steps like 1, 2, 5, 10, 20).
